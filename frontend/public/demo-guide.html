Attendance & Academic Portal — How to Use It

A quick walkthrough for every role. Follow the steps for your part, and you'll be running sessions, marking attendance, and pulling reports in minutes.

📘 Teacher 🪷 BV Leader 🎓 Student

🔑 Signing in

  • Staff (Admin / Teacher / BV Leader): sign in with your username & password, or with Google.
  • First-time password: the default is 1896 — change it via Forgot password? on the login screen (sends a reset link to your email).
  • Students: sign in with Google, or the username/password given by the admin.
  • Dual role? If you are both a Teacher and a BV Leader, use the role switcher in the top bar to move between the two portals.

FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions.

What's the default password and how do I change it?

New staff accounts start with 1896. Change it by clicking Forgot password? on the login page — you'll get a reset link by email (valid for 1 hour, single use). Admins can also reset any user back to 1896.

I'm both a Teacher and a BV Leader. How do I switch?

Use the role switcher in the top-right of the dashboard. It only appears if your account has a second role. A brief loading animation shows while it swaps you into the other portal.

How is BV Leader attendance different from class attendance?

A teacher's session is tied to a subject and counts toward that subject's attendance %. A BV Leader runs one common weekly session for all their students regardless of subject — it's tracked separately and never affects academic percentages.

Why does a student show no BV Leader in my teacher view?

They haven't been assigned one for that subject yet. Expand the class roster and pick a BV Leader from the dropdown, then click Assign — it syncs with the admin's Class BV Leaders page instantly.

A student's email is missing — can I still import them?

Yes. Bulk import keeps rows without an email (a placeholder is generated). Only staff with a real email can use self-service password reset, though.

Can I fix an attendance mistake after closing a session?

Teachers raise an attendance-correction request; the admin approves it. This keeps an audit trail of every change.
